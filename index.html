What the monitor shows

Overnight funding markets set the price of cash for the whole financial system. The monitor gathers the official public data in one place and updates it every weekday, so it is easy to see when cash is getting scarce or plentiful:

  • Rates and spreads — SOFR and the tri-party, broad and dealer GC repo rates relative to the Fed's interest on reserves (IORB), the ON RRP floor and the Standing Repo Facility.
  • Liquidity — bank reserves, the Treasury General Account, ON RRP and SRF usage, and what moved reserves each week.
  • Supply — estimated Treasury net issuance by settlement date and month, and the history of net marketable issuance.
  • Patterns — how repo behaves during GSE cash week and around month- and quarter-ends.

Sources: Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Federal Reserve Board (via FRED), U.S. Treasury (FiscalData, TreasuryDirect, daily yield curve), Office of Financial Research and DTCC. Figures are estimates for information only, not investment advice.
