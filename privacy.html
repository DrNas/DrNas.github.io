Privacy policy

Applies to manasbafna.com and funding.manasbafna.com. Last updated October 29, 2026.

What we collect

These sites have no accounts or sign-up forms and do not ask for personal information. The Funding Markets Monitor stores your display settings (for example chart ranges and your choice of data source) in your own browser's local storage; they never leave your device.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to these sites and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Hosting and data sources

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. When the monitor is set to fetch data live, your browser requests public data directly from the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, the U.S. Treasury, the Office of Financial Research and DTCC, or through a proxy on Cloudflare; those services may log the request.
